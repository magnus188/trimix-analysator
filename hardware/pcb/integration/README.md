## Coax above AO2 — current placement

J402 is now above J401, matching the requested connector order. Their physical centres are respectively **(4.45, 16.60)** and **(4.90, 27.00) mm** in local KiCad coordinates; J401 pin 1 is at (4.90, 24.46). The narrow upper PCB section was widened from 8.60 to **8.90 mm**, retaining at least 0.50 mm copper-to-edge clearance. Four nearby passives were repositioned. The [independent board audit](verification/connector-swap/final-independent-audit.md) checks the exact six allowed placements, outline changes, all 365 pin/net assignments and all 133 values.

The enclosure exterior and both screw datums are preserved. The carrier has a [2 mm dropped floor and end walls](verification/connector-swap/carrier-applied.json) beneath the new coax opening. This maintains one connected carrier without obstructing the button or changing the removal prerequisites. Actual mated elbow and J401 plug geometry remains an open fit check; putting J402 above J401 is not itself a cable-clearance approval.
