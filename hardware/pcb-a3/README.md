# A3 PCB integration — work in progress

The USB daughterboard is routed and passes the saved-board KiCad checks. The main board is a **mechanical placement candidate, still unrouted**. The enclosure showcase, animation and presentation work are paused while PCB design and insertion take priority. These files are engineering drafts, not a fabrication release.

| Item | Current state |
|---|---|
| Main board | Four copper layers, 1.60 mm nominal thickness; 119 original electrical footprints, 12 test pads and two mounting holes. Components on F.Cu face the enclosure rear. No tracks or copper planes yet. |
| USB daughterboard | Two copper layers, 0.60 mm nominal thickness, 16 × 15.72 mm overall outline; four footprints, 15 track segments, four routing vias and two ground fills. |
| Test pads | Twelve bare Ø1 mm pads added to the main schematic; original pin/net assignments preserved and the test-point ERC report contains zero violations. |
| Manufacturing and physical fit | Pending actual parts, mating connectors, cables, solder protrusion, fabrication stack and prototype checks. |

The authoritative editable sources are [the main KiCad project](../kicad/analyzer/Trimix_Analyzer.kicad_pro) and [the USB KiCad project](../kicad/usb-input/Trimix_USB_Input.kicad_pro). The separate [USB board](../kicad/usb-input/Trimix_USB_Input.kicad_pcb) uses a GCT USB4720-03-A receptacle, independent 5.1 kΩ CC resistors and a two-wire power output. USB data pins are unused; this board does not negotiate USB Power Delivery.

The [main placement receipt](verification/main-final-verification.json) records zero native courtyard collisions, zero courtyards outside the outline, and zero copper-edge clearances below 0.50 mm. Schematic parity and the latest full ERC are zero. Main DRC remains open: **288 unconnected items and 39 fabrication-rule findings**—15 package-pad gaps of 0.15 mm against the current 0.20 mm rule, and 24 thermal drills of 0.20 mm against the current 0.30 mm minimum. Rules have not been relaxed. The [main placement STEP](exports/Trimix_Analyzer_A3_Placement.step) is a mechanical preview, with provisional purchased-part models.

The USB footprint lands and 0.60 ± 0.10 mm board requirement follow the [GCT Rev B drawing](https://gct.co/files/drawings/usb4720.pdf). The implemented Edge.Cuts and registration are recorded in [usb-layout.json](verification/usb-layout.json). Its front clearance chamfer remains provisional. The initial layout receipt deliberately retains its earlier board hash and “pending DRC” status; [usb-final-verification.json](verification/usb-final-verification.json) binds the current board, sources and STEP hashes and records the later independent check: **zero DRC violations, zero unconnected items and zero schematic parity issues**, under the project’s recorded check configuration.

The [USB STEP](exports/Trimix_USB_Input.step) includes the board, copper and resistor models. It has no exact connector or cable body. The current Fusion comparison reports no intersections with printed parts, housing or fasteners in its candidate set; 26 intersections remain against the earlier simplified connector shell/rear insulator. **Fit to the actual connector is unresolved.** This is not an overall Fusion fit pass.

The main outline and two Ø2.30 mm mounting holes come from the A3 carrier datums, including the button and USB-service notches. [Mechanical interface notes](verification/MECHANICAL_INTERFACE.md) provide the original coordinate transform and height limits. The Fusion carrier now has [11 lead-clearance windows](verification/carrier-relief-applied.json) and a local 0.60 mm outward rim extension to X81.20 mm. The revised carrier remains one solid and its [native static comparison](verification/carrier-fusion-interference.json) reports zero intersections and zero calculation errors. The earlier enclosure print-release meshes and slicer projects have not been regenerated for this changed carrier. Actual solder protrusion and stiffness remain separate checks; the earlier full main-board interference report predates this relief.

The [native service check](verification/pcb-service-check.json) verifies a continuous 27 mm rearward extraction of the board and carrier, plus access for both Ø5 × 50 mm driver shafts. This requires the rear cover off, battery disconnected, all PCB harnesses detached and both carrier screws removed; the battery holder stays installed. The check covers rigid modeled parts, not real wire bends, solder, hands or fit tolerances. [Final checks](verification/final-checks.json) collect the evidence and remaining gates. The [native Fusion archive](exports/Trimix_Enclosure_A3_PCBFit.f3d) and [STEP assembly](exports/Trimix_Enclosure_A3_PCBFit.step) are saved; their hashes and archive integrity were checked. The [delivery receipt](verification/fusion-delivery.json) records a successful native reopen with 829 solids and zero total-volume difference, plus review of the [actual rear assembly view](exports/PCB-in-enclosure-rear.png). These checks preserve the modeled-geometry limits above.

The [test-point receipt](verification/testpoints-verification.json) maps TP1001–TP1012 to ground, input/battery/system/host rails, enable, I²C and sensor measurement nodes. Probe access must be preserved during routing. The [footprint review](verification/footprint-review.md) and [TI DSJ receipt](verification/u201-ti-dsj-footprint.json) separate manufacturer dimensions from remaining package, stencil and assembly decisions.

Before fabrication, complete main routing and its power-loop, thermal and analogue review; confirm purchased inductors and mating connectors; reconcile trustworthy connector geometry; and check actual wires, solder tails, strain relief, service access and source/cable behavior on a prototype. Zero DRC does not establish charging performance or physical fit.
