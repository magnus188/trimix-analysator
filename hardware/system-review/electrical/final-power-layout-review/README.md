## Switching, capacitors and ground

BQ SW→L101 is entirely F.Cu, with no SW vias or parallel inner-layer power path. L101.2→C104.1 has a direct 4.5358 mm/1.0 mm F witness. The bank is **C104+C105, 44 µF nominal**, plus distributed C201/C202 and the newer local C1084.7 µF. U101.15→C108.1 is 1.5375 mm F. The longer SYS sense/battery/distribution routes must not be mistaken for the complete buck switching hot loop. Nothing here supports adding another arbitrary bulk capacitor; [the existing capacitance audit](../current-path-review/charger-output-audit/README.md) distinguishes typical derating estimates from guaranteed effective capacitance.

U201's two inductor networks remain F-only with no vias. Input C201, output C204–C206 and L201 retain compact front-layer connections: U201.10→C201.1 is 2.6063 mm; U201.4→C204.1 is 2.8374 mm; the two inductor witnesses are 2.85/2.20 mm. Individual pad escapes remain narrower than the power spines. C202 is distributed bulk, not a substitute for C201. FB/VINA and ground connections are present. This accords better with the placement intent in [TI TPS63020 §10.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps63020.pdf), without establishing ripple, stability, current capability or temperature.

The [final root ground/path review](../routing-candidate/final-cleanup/root-review/README.md), independently checked against this source, retains all29 load/monitor witnesses. In1 is one grounded region with105 annular contacts; In2 has eleven grounded regions with21 contacts and is **not continuous**. The final U115 ground barrel (20.75,88.3), C114 ground barrel (11.1,91.1) and C115 ground barrel (12.8,93.95) occupy different local In2 islands, all connected to In1. Global ground continuity does not establish an ideal Kelvin/star return or bound parasitic coupling. Actual ripple/noise remains a separate test.

## USB protection and thermal paths

U115 has C115100 nF input bypass and C1144.7 µF shared output/U114-input capacitance. Input bypass has a 3.5286 mm B witness; output→C114 takes 13.3633 mm over B/F with two barrels. U115 GND→nearest witnessed grounded barrel is 5.347 mm B. The control resistor/capacitor paths are locally connected, but these distances and the split local In2 regions keep bypass/ground transient impedance unqualified. U114 has nearby R119, while its output uses the remotely connected C101; its output→C101 witness is 15.0107 mm F/In2, two barrels. These are genuine limitations to include in the **existing EL11/EL13 source-transition tests**, not a new claim of 6.396 V failure. [TI TPS25947 §8.4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf), [TI TPS22950-Q1 §9.4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22950-q1.pdf).

U101 and U201 exposed pads are grounded and have respectively9 and15 package thermal bores. The [separate27-hole process packet](../routing-candidate/final-cleanup/fill-cap-process/README.md) explicitly requires filled, planarized, copper-capped treatment of these24 holes plus3signal VIPPO sites; supplier acceptance and solder-void/flatness results remain pending. U115's heat path is through its IN/OUT lands, not an invented grounded exposed pad; U114 and U701 likewise do not have the U101/U201 exposed-pad arrangement. Geometry/via counts do not determine junction temperature. EL10 still needs the real source/load envelope, PCB copper thermal analysis and enclosure measurements; no current or thermal rating is inferred from DRC.

## Disposition

The four targeted refinements above should be explicitly resolved before ordering, rather than hidden inside the broad EL10 hold. The owner is preparing isolated trials; **this report accepts no new geometry**. Any accepted correction needs native clearance/parity, source-bound conductor/ground witnesses, updated CAM/BOM/process checks and mechanical clearance revalidation. The completed9f274 CAM checkpoint remains intact.

Existing EL10 covers load/thermal and broad ripple testing; EL11 covers the unbounded OVP/cable/short transient envelope; EL13 covers cold/depleted startup. Those holds remain necessary, but do not themselves close avoidable bootstrap/feedback/local-capacitor layout work. No physical test, factory upload, order or release occurred.
