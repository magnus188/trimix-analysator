   FPF2286 is an OVP with integrated low on-resistance single channel
switch. The device contains an N-MOSFET that can operate over an                       MARKING DIAGRAM
input voltage range of 2.8 V to 23 V and can support a maximum
continuous current of 4 A.                                                                         3FMG
   When the input voltage exceeds the over-voltage threshold, the
internal FET is turned off immediately to prevent damage to the                      3F   = Specific Device Code
protected downstream components.                                                     M    = Month Code
   FPF2286 is available in a small 6-bump WLCSP package and operate                  G    = Pb-Free Package
over the free-air temperature range of −40 °C to +85 °C.
