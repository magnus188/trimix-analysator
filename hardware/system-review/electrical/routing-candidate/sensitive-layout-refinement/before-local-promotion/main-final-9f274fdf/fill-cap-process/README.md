# Selected-hole fill/cap requirement — quotation pending

The 9f274fdf frozen board requires epoxy resin fill, planarization and copper cap on **both faces of all 27 holes** in `required-fill-cap-holes.csv`: 9 U101 thermal bores, 15 U201 thermal bores and 3 separately reviewed signal VIPPO. The visual map identifies the coordinates; the CSV also gives exact native identities and bore diameters. Do not fill connector/component-lead holes.

This closes the ambiguity about the package thermal holes being encoded as plated pads rather than routed vias. Twelve of the 24 thermal bores intersect the current thermal paste windows. Filling all 24 avoids relying on window alignment to prevent solder loss through the remaining holes. This is the chosen engineering process requirement, approved for supplier review, not evidence that a factory accepted or performed it. Ordinary open or soldermask-plugged bores are not substitutes.

TI requires the BQ25895 power pad to be soldered to board ground, with sufficient thermal vias connecting underlying ground; its general layout guidance also requires short decoupling connections. [BQ25895 Rev C, section 11.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25895.pdf). TPS63020 thermal guidance calls for soldering the exposed pad to improve PCB coupling. [TPS63020 Rev I, section 10.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps63020.pdf). These sources justify retaining thermal connections. They do not qualify this particular stencil, bore count, solder voiding or assembled temperature rise.

JLC describes epoxy fill followed by copper capping for solderable pad holes and requests a coordinate/image instruction identifying selected holes. Its ordinary via-finish options do not automatically apply to plated component pads. [Via-covering process](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-via-covering). Its published POFV option covers 0.20–0.50 mm holes, with a 0.05 mm minimum annulus and extra charges for four layers. [POFV requirements](https://jlcpcb.com/news/free-via-in-pad-6-20-layer-pcbs-pofv). The quoted drill tools, filled-hole classification, cap thickness/flatness, ENIG, solder volume, void acceptance and double-sided reflow must be approved for this exact job. A generic online capability is not that approval.

The separate `../factory-stencil/` source removes paste only from manual, DNP and PCB-feature populations. It retains the 24 factory components' original apertures, including the thermal windows. Independent exported-geometry comparison is required. C706 has no aperture in this factory subset. Stencil thickness, reduction, panel registration, cap flatness and joint inspection remain assembler gates; these files are not an approved stencil or assembly order.

No routed source, copper, package or mechanical geometry is changed by this process supplement.
