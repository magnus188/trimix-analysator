## Corrections implemented

- U401/U502 remain ADS122C04IPWR; AO2, JJ-CCR and MD62 sensors are unchanged. Helium is physically **HE_REF minus HE_SENSE**. R506/R507 are680ohm0.1%; He acquisition profile2/O2profile1.
- U501/U702 now TPS7A2030PDBVR with correct pin4NC. C503/C706 are DNP. Selected effective capacitance exceeds the documented0.47uF output requirement under the stated engineering deratings.
- U302 LM66100 supplies HOST_5V and blocks reverse current toward our VOUT_5V converter. This does not establish every Guition USB/battery backfeed path.
- BQ D+/D- remain NC, preventing its autonomous HVDCP/MAXC voltage negotiation. TUSB320LAI detects USB-C advertisements; PI3USB9201 detects legacy BC1.2. Daughterboard harness is six conductors. The old discrete Rd R901/R902 have been removed because the sink controller supplies dead-battery Rd. The USB board has dedicated four-line signal ESD and VBUS ESD parts; their clamping does not establish survival of every downstream surge.
- The unsupported4.02k BQ ILIM extrapolation was rejected: BQ specifies no ILIM operation below500mA. U114 is the exact **TPS22950CQDDCRQ1** leaded Q1 variant, with sourced19.1k0.1% default resistor and qualified parallel1k branch. Its manufacturer34/50/66mA table applies at19.2k; the19.1k substitution has only a typical-formula estimate, with current corners and transient overshoot still unmeasured. BQ R103300ohm is a supported-range backup.
- U112 authorization latch plus two series FETs require a fresh host edge after VBUS/CC/fault reset. U113 senses the protected input independently of firmware. Q112 exposes actual latch reset through shared GPIO49/CHG_INT_N. GPIO52 stays unused.
- U115 TPS259470ARPWR adds sustained-input OVLO with fixed precision resistors. Its AUXOFF, not FLT, clears permission. Physical fast-surge/ESD/inrush qualification remains open; the6V-absolute downstream limiter is not proven to survive every pulse.
- Critical capacitor packages/banks and inductors were replaced with exact sourced parts and maximum-height envelopes. Optional SW101 was replaced by local QON service pad TP1014, referenced to TP1001 GND; the external power button is unchanged. J402 is the specified Amphenol142138 SMB jack with a manufacturer-drawing reconstruction; original connector poses remain fixed.

## Before ordering

Confirm the fabricator stackup and factory assembly process for exact lands, three signal VIPPO sites and thermal vias; validate actual mechanical mates and harness envelopes; establish cell identity/current/temperature limits and real pack NTC; qualify unresolved protection/current/transient assumptions. Stock, price and assembly quotations are not confirmed. No physical test is marked performed. The bundle includes 169-reference assembly and marking maps, with exact purchasing, factory, manual, DNP and PCB-feature lists. The full-board paste export is diagnostic; the factory subset stencil still needs assembler approval.

The optional internal charger LED D101/R106 was removed to make room for C108, a supplementary 4.7 µF local SYS bypass. Both qualified C104/C105 bulk capacitors remain. TP1013 exposes charger STAT on the rear copper; remove the board for probing. TP1014 retains manual QON wake/reset access close to U101. The local service pads use the assembly reference drawing where dense silk would be unreadable. Probe fit and physical fault recovery remain pending. See `charger-bypass-change.json` and `qon-service-change.json`.

C101 now uses an actual KEMET C0603C105K4RACTU 1 µF, 16 V, X7R 0603 part. This retains the nominal 1 µF BQ input-bypass function while allowing a short front-copper connection beside U101. C102 remains the selected 22 µF 1210 PMID bulk capacitor. The local source change is recorded in `local-input-cap-change.json`; `routing-candidate/local-cap-loop-witnesses.json` records explicit copper paths, without claiming measured ripple, inductance or thermal performance. `standard-part-selections.json` is refreshed from the exported schematic by `refresh_selection_snapshot.py`, so removed LED/switch parts and the superseded Harwin host header do not remain in the active selection snapshot.

The final local refinement places real factory 0402 C103 (TDK C1005X7R1H473K050BE, 47 nF/50 V) close to BTST/SW, and factory 0805 C107 (TDK C2012X5R1A476M125AC, 47 µF/10 V) close to REGN. CO feedback and output-capacitor paths are shorter, with a direct front-copper output-capacitor return. The CO switch route and upper feedback leg grew modestly; the [source-bound comparison](routing-candidate/sensitive-layout-refinement/frozen-local-bundle/README.md) states these tradeoffs. Only the explicitly checked bootstrap branch uses rear switch-net copper; the BQ load-current SW path stays on F.Cu. R504 is a manual rear component, installed before the carrier. Factory/manual counts are 27/118; 141 printed references and 28 assembly-only locators cover all 169 features. Capacitor effective values are curve-based estimates, and startup, ripple, noise and thermal behavior remain unmeasured.
