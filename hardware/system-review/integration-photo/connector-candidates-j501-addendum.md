# J501 cable height — bounded research addendum, 8 September 2026

**The most practical candidate is a factory-assembled Samtec IDSS single-row cable with a shorter-post vertical header.** It saves about 4.7 mm of axial housing height and already sends the wires sideways. A right-angle header remains an alternative if horizontal service space exists. Neither proposal is adopted: the native PCB, CAD, purchasing BOM, J401/J402 positions and firmware were not changed.

## Preferred candidate for a later fit check

The exact catalog-configurable example is **IDSS-03-S-03.00-T**: three positions, a single female end, 3.00 inch cable length, tin contacts, ordinary color-coded cable, with no blocked cavity or reverse-wiring option. This is supported by the [IDSS catalog](https://suddendocs.samtec.com/catalog_english/idss.pdf) and [IDSX assembly print, revision AE](https://suddendocs.samtec.com/prints/idsx-xx-x-xx.xx-xxx-xxx-mkt.pdf). The exact tin example's product page could not be retrieved; its length, price, availability and factory order acceptance remain to be confirmed. A 76.2 ±3.18 mm example is **not** a measured harness length or a purchase recommendation.

The print distinguishes short connectors from the generic catalog illustration. For two, three or four positions, housing height is **9.144–9.271 mm**. Three-position row length is **7.976–8.484 mm**; single-row thickness is 2.54 mm catalog reference. The cable leaves perpendicular to the mating posts, close to the cap, and the brown wire identifies contact 1. The supplied wire is 28 AWG, seven strands, tinned copper/PVC. There is no positive latch to an ordinary bare-pin header. The print's minimum contact withdrawal is about 0.56 N under its specified test-post conditions; it does not prove installed vibration retention. Do not transfer the catalog's **IDMD** 3 A rating to this IDSS cable.

The existing Harwin post range, 5.85–6.35 mm, does **not** fit entirely inside IDSS's specified insertion range, **5.588–6.223 mm**. A shorter housing cannot be bottomed indiscriminately onto the existing header. No post cutting or speculative printed shim is proposed here.

For J501, the exact vertical board candidate is [**HTSW-103-07-T-S**](https://www.samtec.com/products/htsw-103-07-t-s). The [HTSW print, revision BQ](https://suddendocs.samtec.com/prints/htsw-xxx-xx-xxx-x-xx-xx-xx-mkt.pdf), figure 1/table 1, specifies a **5.842 ±0.203 mm** post for this lead style: 5.639–6.045 mm, within the IDSS insertion interval. The finish is matte tin and the post is nominally 0.635 mm square. The header body is 2.54 mm **reference**, so the calculated seated stack is **11.684–11.811 mm plus the header body's unspecified reference-dimension tolerance**. It is not a guaranteed maximum stack. Side-exiting cable avoids adding an immediate axial wire bend. A complete sample/mate model must still account for tilt, tolerances, strain relief and withdrawal.

The [manufacturer cable print, revision AE](https://suddendocs.samtec.com/prints/cb-xx-28-7-36-xxx-x.pdf) bounds the ordinary colored cable's insulated-conductor thickness at **1.143 mm maximum**. It provides no minimum bend radius. Its 2.54 mm conductor spacing is not the bundle's circular diameter; joining, separating, wrapping or sealing the wires changes the route. Its cable data lists 1 A nominal at 10 °C, not a universal assembly current guarantee. Verify the MD62 supply drop/noise and actual far-end termination. Neither that sensor termination nor the AO2's existing cable has been measured or replaced.

## Right-angle alternative

Exact primary product pages exist for [**HTSW-103-08-T-S-RA**](https://www.samtec.com/products/htsw-103-08-t-s-ra) and [**HTSW-102-08-T-S-RA**](https://www.samtec.com/products/htsw-102-08-t-s-ra). These use a 5.842 +0.127/−0.203 mm post (BQ table 1 footnote 7), compatible axially with the 5.50–6.30 mm Harwin M20-106 insertion range. Thus the three-pin option can retain **M20-1060300 plus three M20-1180046 tin contacts**, and the two-pin option can use **M20-1060200 plus two such contacts**. This still requires a qualified crimp or vendor-built cable.

Figure 5 gives the RA single-row body height as **3.048 mm reference** and the solder tail as **2.286 mm reference**. Its horizontal mating face is about 4.06 mm from the hole row, using the reference 2.54 mm insulator depth and 1.52 mm tail setback. A 14 mm Harwin female therefore extends about **18.06 mm horizontally from the hole row**, before wire bend, handling and withdrawal. This is a reference estimate, not a tolerance-qualified envelope. The print does not independently dimension the post centre above the seating plane; do not assume that the whole Harwin female clears the PCB merely because the male body is low. The catalog also shows 3.09 mm for the HTSW RA body, so the exact selected part's model/dimensional confirmation must resolve reference rounding before close-clearance work.

The [HTSW footprint, revision B](https://suddendocs.samtec.com/prints/htsw-xxx-xx-xxx-x-xx-xx-xx-footprint.pdf), shows one row of 2.54 mm holes for the single-row versions. Its RA illustration places the socket-facing body at the PCB edge. Same hole spacing is not proof of same occupied footprint. A horizontal mate needs a new courtyard/model and clearance from nearby components and the housing.

## Integration decisions still required

- **Keep a complete cable envelope.** The proposed vertical IDSS solution uses roughly 8.5 mm maximum row length and 9.271 mm maximum socket height, but its transverse body dimension and header base remain reference values. Add deliberate clearance, not fictitious manufacturer maxima.
- **Check the plated holes and tails.** Current J501/J401/J103/J801/J802 holes are nominally 1.00 mm. Samtec recommends 1.02 mm holes (catalog ±0.03 mm). A fabrication allowance as wide as ±0.08 mm does not stay within that recommendation. Check finished-hole capability, square-tail fit, annular rings, pad spacing and solder protrusion rather than declaring a drop-in substitution. Style 07 has a 2.54 mm reference tail; style 08 RA has a shorter 2.286 mm reference tail.
- **Preserve net identity.** Each single-row terminal continues to its corresponding PCB hole; turning the header or changing wire exit does not authorize reversing nets. A bare-pin mate remains reversible. Specify and test contact-to-net continuity and physical pin-1 orientation separately at each endpoint.
- **Treat the other headers individually.** J401 also uses the three-pin Harwin header; J103/J801/J802 use the two-pin version. [HTSW-102-07-T-S](https://www.samtec.com/products/htsw-102-07-t-s) is the analogous vertical header candidate. IDSS supports two positions, but cable length, gauge/current, pin map and endpoint retention must be qualified per circuit. J104 remains an intentionally open commissioning header. Preserve the approved J401/J402 positions and the unmeasured AO2 cable until a complete candidate is reviewed.
- **Keep fabrication and physical tests pending.** No connector insertion, pull, bend, solder, continuity, current, leakage or native interference/removal test was performed for these alternatives. No vendor was contacted or order placed.

## Other candidates screened

TE **3-640442-3** (26 AWG) and **3-640441-3** (24 AWG) are exact tin MTA-100 closed-end IDC parts with side cable exit. The [640442 drawing](https://www.te.com/commerce/DocumentDelivery/DDEController?Action=srchrtrv&DocFormat=pdf&DocLang=English&DocNm=640442&DocType=Customer+Drawing&PartCntxt=3-640442-3) and [application 114-1019](https://www.te.com/commerce/DocumentDelivery/DDEController?Action=srchrtrv&DocFormat=pdf&DocLang=English&DocNm=114-1019&DocType=Specification+Or+Standard&PartCntxt=3-640442-3) support 0.64 ±0.03 mm posts and controlled IDC termination. The manufacturer's product dimensions are 13.21 mm height, 6.99 mm width and 7.62 mm length. **6.99 mm must not be advertised as its installed axial height.** A permitted min/max insertion depth into the existing 6.1 mm posts was not established. Its locking ramp also requires a matching header, absent from the existing bare-pin part. It is not a qualified lower mate here.

Harwin **M20-9960346** is explicitly obsolete in the [M20-996 drawing, issue 24](https://content.harwin.com/asset/ef546d83-b062-4ce5-b3eb-0eb32bebaecb/DRG-00475-Technical-Drawing-Datasheet-M20-996-pdf.pdf) and [manufacturer discontinuance notice](https://cdn.harwin.com/pdfs/Obsolescence_notification_10-06-2019_Harwin.pdf). The active three-pin **M20-9960345** is gold, and keeps the same 6.1 ±0.25 mm post-length mismatch. It is not the recommended tin replacement. Samtec SMSS has a 12.45 mm reference body, standard counts other than three and no catalog tin option establishing this requested exact mate; no custom combination was invented.
